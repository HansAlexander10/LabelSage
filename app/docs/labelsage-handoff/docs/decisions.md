# Decisions

The decisions behind the MVP design. The team makes decisions; the coding agent builds them. If a build needs a new decision, add it to `open-questions.md` instead of this file.

| # | Decision | Why | Where it shows | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Home is the AI chat.** Onboarding ends on "Check my first product", which opens the camera. The chat start screen leads with "Check a product". | People open the app to check something now. The chat holds context (who, what they avoid, the last product) so follow-ups like "what about Leo?" work. | Chat, Splash, onboarding summary | Oct 2026 |
| D2 | **Lists, basket, restock and Instacart are parked.** | Focus the MVP on checking. They return after the MVP. | Parked boards on the canvas | Oct 2026 |
| D3 | **Severity per allergen, Allergy by default.** Allergy: the ingredients, plus "may contain" when the person's switch is on. Intolerance: the ingredients only. | One person can be allergic to peanut and intolerant to lactose. The more careful default for a new user is Allergy. | Onboarding "Allergy or intolerance?", Family cards, chat change confirm | Oct 2026 |
| D4 | **Product data and availability source: OPEN.** | Needs a commercial and legal choice. | `ProductSource` protocol, fixture source | Open |
| D5 | **Share is cut from the MVP.** | Not needed to check a product; adds privacy questions. | Removed from the product page and Compare | Oct 2026 |
| D6 | **The store stays; the region is required.** Onboarding asks "Where are you?" (location or city/ZIP, required) and "Where do you shop?" (one store near that ZIP, optional: "Set up later") once for the household, after everyone's allergies, before the summary. Family > Settings can change both. | Products and stores differ by area; "Other options" must be things you can buy where you shop. | Onboarding steps 12 and 13, summary row, Family > Settings | 23 Oct 2026 |
| D7 | **AI-first flows: chat, then camera, then product sheet, then compare.** | Matches D1; every story on the Flows board starts in the chat. | Flows board | Oct 2026 |
| D8 | **Code decides, LabelSage explains.** The deterministic engine sets the flag. The on-device model writes the summary from the engine's findings and may replace the reason line in place, never the flag. Without the model, the deterministic reason stays. | A verdict about an allergen must be reproducible, testable and work offline. | Product page loading note, chat answers | Oct 2026 |
| D9 | **Only one LabelSage on screen.** | He is one character. Two at once reads as a glitch and splits attention. | Every screen; chat dock vs sheets | Oct 2026 (hard rule) |
| D10 | **Icons from Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D (MIT), bundled.** The nearest icon stands in where none exists (tree nuts use the chestnut, sesame the bagel, mustard the hot dog, mussels the oyster). No generated icons. | One free library with a single 3D style and point of view; licence allows bundling. | Icon library board, onboarding, Family | 23 Oct 2026 |
| D11 | **App and mascot names come from one config value** (`Brand.app`, `Brand.mascot`). | The name may change after testing. | Everywhere | Oct 2026 |
| D12 | **Avoid vs Prefer not.** Avoid hides it (Not for). Prefer not only shows it lower (Check first). Allergies, medical diets and diets are always Avoid; the "Anything else" groups default to Avoid, or Prefer not when the only reason given is "I just don’t like some foods". The person can flip each one. | People avoid some things strictly and others loosely. | Onboarding summary, Family cards | Oct 2026 |
| D13 | **Verdicts name the person.** "OK for Mia · Not for Sam", never "Good" or "Bad". "OK for everyone" when three or more people are all OK. | A verdict is about a person, not a judgment of the food. | Flags everywhere | Oct 2026 |
| D14 | **Profile changes from the chat need a confirm.** LabelSage shows the exact change ("Add sesame to Mia’s allergies? Allergy or Intolerance"), nothing is saved until Confirm, then he checks that person's go-tos again. | A wrong inference about an allergy is costly. | Chat "Change allergies" | Oct 2026 |
| D16 | **The name is LabelSage** (app and mascot), replacing LabelWard. | Artem, 23 Oct 2026. | `Brand.app`, `Brand.mascot`; every screen and doc | 23 Oct 2026 |
| D15 | **Go-tos are checked again every time** the app opens and after any profile change. If the ingredients changed: "Heads up: Mia’s granola changed. It now contains egg." | Quiet recipe changes are a known risk for allergy households. | Family > Go-tos, chat | Oct 2026 |
