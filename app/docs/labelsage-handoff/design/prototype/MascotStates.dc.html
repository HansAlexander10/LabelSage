It reads the label so you don’t have to. Thirteen states, one rig, each a clear action that loops seamlessly with the feet on the ground or seated on books (only “done” leaves it, on purpose: a jump, then hops). Motion is transform and opacity only, and Reduce Motion holds each pose still. On an allergy result he stands guard: still and serious, and the flag leads.

Idle
Home and empty states. Feet planted: breathes and shifts its weight, glances left and right, blinks; arms, cape and string follow a beat later. 4.8 s loop. After 15 s without a tap he starts humming (wait and see); a tap stops it.
Reading
While a photo of the ingredients is read. Looks through the magnifier with one eye, then moves the lens across to the other eye and back; whichever eye is under the glass shows big, the arm stretches to follow. Lines turn green as items are found. 3.2 s loop.
Done
Once, when reading finishes: squash, jump, land. Then a happy hop from one leg to the other with a pause on each: stands on one leg, crouches, hops, lands on the other. Arms wave, sparkles twinkle. 2 s loop.
Thinking
When the agent needs a choice (which pesto?). Feet planted: one arm folded, the other hand under the chin tapping twice; rocks gently, eyes drift up left and right, brow lifts, mouth goes “hmm”; the ? bobs. 3.2 s loop.
