Light glass (controls)

Floating action bar, composer, nav buttons over content: white at 62%, 24px blur, 180% saturation, 1px white edge. Follows iOS Liquid Glass: controls float, content stays flat.

Dark panels (standouts)

Compare verdicts and scan results sit on an opaque dark panel with one verdict-colored glow. Content, not glass: per the HIG, Liquid Glass stays on the control layer.

Sheets

Detailed states open as native sheets with a grabber: medium detent for a product or verdict, large for a comparison. Swipe down or tap the scrim to return to the chat.
