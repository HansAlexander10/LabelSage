Rules
Hard rule: only one {{name}}
He is one person, so never two on a screen. In the chat he stands above the composer; when a sheet or tip needs him, he moves there and leaves his spot. Two at once is a bug.
Who speaks
{{name}}, always beside the message. The bubble’s tail points at him, so it’s clear the agent is talking.
When
A 3-step tour on first run. One tip the first time each screen opens, shown once. New features. Empty states.
How long
One idea per message. Title up to 5 words, text under 20. Plain words, no puns near health.
Buttons
Next or Got it (primary). Skip ends the whole tour. Tips never cover a flag or block a decision.
Never

Accessible
Announced as a dialog; focus starts on the message. Reduce Motion keeps him still. “Show tips again” lives in Profile.
