# Privacy Policy for LabelSage

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

## Overview
LabelSage is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your family's health data stays on your phone. We don't collect, store, or share personal information on our servers.

## What data does LabelSage collect?

### On your device (not sent anywhere)
* Family member names and their dietary restrictions (allergies, intolerances, diets, religious rules)
* The products you scan and check
* Your search history within the app
* Your preferences and settings

All of this data stays on your phone only. It is never sent to our servers or any third party.

### The product catalog
LabelSage includes a catalog of about 1 million US food products. This catalog is downloaded to your device once and searched locally. The catalog data comes from three public sources:
* **Open Food Facts** (licensed under ODbL)
* **FoodData Central** (USDA, CC0)
* **openFDA** (public domain)

We do not send information about which products you scan to our servers.

## What we do NOT collect
* Location data
* Device identifiers or advertising ID
* Browsing history outside the app
* Any information about your contacts
* Biometric data
* Any analytics or tracking

## Third-party services
LabelSage does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. The app does not embed analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising frameworks.

The product catalog data is sourced from public databases (see above), but we do not share user data with these sources.

## Updates to the catalog
When you update LabelSage, a new product catalog may be downloaded. This is a data transfer only — it does not include any information about the products you have previously scanned.

## Children's privacy
LabelSage is intended for families, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 beyond what parents or guardians enter into their family profiles. All data stays on-device and is never shared.

## Security
Your family's data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS encryption (Data Protection). We recommend:
* Keeping your device updated
* Using Face ID or a strong passcode
* Not sharing access to your device

## Changes to this policy
We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be released with the next app version and posted here. Your continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new policy.

## Beta status
LabelSage is currently in beta. The allergen-matching rules are awaiting final clinician review before launch. **Do not rely on LabelSage as your only check for a serious allergy — always read the label yourself.**

## Questions
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us through the app.

---

> **No data leaves your phone. Your privacy is not negotiable.**
